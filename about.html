Jaipur Royal Roots

The Master Artisans of Meenakari & Kundan

Every piece in our royal collection begins with master karigars whose families have served the royal courts of Jaipur and Mewar for generations.

In an era dominated by mass machine stamping, we maintain slow jewelry principles: hand-beaten gold foils, natural vitreous mineral powders, and kiln firings that turn pure quartz dust into lustrous jewels.

Three Craft Disciplines

Our Atelier Specializations

1. Pachi Kundan Setting

Uncut stones secured using pure 24K gold foil (kundan) burnished by hand. An open-back technique that catches light with unparalleled sparkle.

2. High-Fire Meenakari

Vitreous mineral glass enamel applied into engraved grooves and fired at 800°C in ceramic kilns to ensure colors never fade or chip.

3. Aerospace PVD Gold

Vacuum plasma deposition that bonds genuine 18K gold atoms to 316L titanium steel for 10x thicker wear resistance that survives daily showers.
