The Openable Screw-Pin Advantage

Traditional non-openable glass or lac bangles force women to squeeze rigid circles over their knuckles, requiring hand lotion or plastic bags to wear. The Vijay Bangles Meera Kadas feature our signature threaded brass screw-pin:

  • Effortless Donning: Opens like a hinged cuff. No pain or knuckle squeeze.
  • Zero Dropping Risk: Threaded screw pin locks securely inside the bangle body.
  • Dual Wearing: Wear as a standalone wrist kada or as side enhancers for red bridal chudas.
