Client Assistance

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a private bridal styling consultation?
You can click on the "Book Bridal Appointment" tab above, or chat with our VIP WhatsApp stylist. We offer both virtual styling sessions (via high-definition video with color swatch previews) and private in-person appointments at our Jaipur and Mumbai salons.
How can I be certain of my bangle or kada size before ordering?
We provide an interactive Bangles Sizing Studio (view sizing guide) with printable measurement templates and wrist circumference calculators. If you are still unsure, our stylists can guide you via WhatsApp, and all kadas come with our complimentary 7-day sizing exchange guarantee.
How are orders dispatched and insured?
Every shipment is packed under tamper-evident holographic security seals and dispatched via specialized high-value logistics partners (Sequel Secure and BlueDart Apex). 100% of the transit value is insured by Aura & Artisan until you sign upon delivery.
Does your 18K anti-tarnish jewelry survive water and perfume?
Yes. Our everyday demi-fine pieces are coated with 10x thicker 18K vacuum PVD gold over surgical-grade 316L stainless steel. They are completely sweatproof, waterproof, and gym-safe with zero green skin discoloration.
