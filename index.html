The Engineering of Everyday Luxury

Why Our 18K Gold Plating Never Fades

Unlike traditional cheap flash plating that rubs off in weeks, we deploy aerospace-grade PVD (Physical Vapor Deposition) technology.

Ordinary Fashion Jewellery

Conventional Flash Plating

  • 0.05 Micron Plating: Wears off within 2 to 4 weeks of regular wear.
  • Base Metal Corrosion: Cheap zinc alloy or copper oxidizes and turns fingers green.
  • Chemical Sensitivity: Ruined immediately by hand sanitizers, lotions, and sweat.
  • Nickel Allergens: Causes redness, itching, and earlobe irritation.
Aura & Artisan Standard

Aerospace 18K PVD Titanium Gold

  • 10x Thicker PVD Coating: Ion-bonded in vacuum chambers for multi-year durability.
  • Medical-Grade 316L Core: Zero rusting, zero corrosion, and 100% hypoallergenic.
  • Shower & Gym Proof: Wear while swimming, working out, or showering with zero worries.
  • Lifetime Warranty: We replace any piece that ever discolors or tarnishes.
