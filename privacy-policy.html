Safeguarding Your Luxury Acquisition Data

At AURA & ARTISAN JEWELLERS PRIVATE LIMITED ("Aura & Artisan", "Maison", "we", "us", or "our"), preserving the sacred trust of our patrons is as foundational as the 400-year-old Rajasthani karigari we uphold. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent practices regarding the collection, protection, and handling of your personal information across our online atelier and physical salons.

1 Information Entrusted to Us

When you explore our bridal heirlooms, order tailored Meenakari bangles, or schedule a salon consultation, we collect only necessary details:

  • Patron Identification: Full name, verified mobile contact, email address, and billing coordinates.
  • Insured Shipping Data: Physical residential or salon delivery address, landmark, and postal code for secure courier dispatch.
  • Artisanal Sizing Archives: Bangle diameter records (2.2 – 2.10), wrist circumferences, and ring measurements logged to expedite future bespoke commissions.
  • Bespoke Bridal Notes: Colorway preferences, wedding dates, and bridal trousseau consultations shared with our Jaipur styling team.

2 256-Bit SSL Financial & Payment Security

AURA & ARTISAN does not store, log, or hold raw credit card credentials, debit card PINs, or UPI security pins on our servers. All digital checkouts are securely handled via RBI-authorized, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways (Razorpay & Cashfree) utilizing military-grade 256-Bit SHA-2 SSL encryption.

3 How Your Information Is Used

  • Order Fulfillment & Transit Insurance: Generating tamper-evident security manifests and coordinating insured BlueDart air couriers.
  • Concierge Updates: Dispatching real-time SMS and WhatsApp consignment tracking links.
  • Lifetime Color Guarantee Verification: Authenticating ownership for lifetime anti-tarnish warranty claims on our 18K PVD collections.
  • Maison Circle Inclusions: Extending private previews to limited handcrafted drops (only with your voluntary opt-in consent).

4 Strict Non-Disclosure Policy

We operate under an unyielding principle: We never sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal or acquisition data to third-party telemarketers or ad brokers. Client data is solely disclosed to vetted service partners strictly bound by non-disclosure agreements: BlueDart Insured Logistics (for physical doorstep handover) and official SMS transactional notification providers.

5 Patron Rights & Privacy Grievance Officer

In compliance with the Information Technology Act 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you retain full rights to inspect, update, or request the permanent deletion of your customer profile at any time.
