Artisanal Excellence, Hallmarking & Purchase Covenant

Welcome to AURA & ARTISAN. By browsing our digital catalog, purchasing our heritage kadas, or ordering custom bridal sets, you agree to comply with the terms and conditions outlined below. These terms safeguard the integrity of living Indian karigari and ensure complete client satisfaction.

1 Hallmarking, Precious Metallurgy & Material Purity

Every piece created under the AURA & ARTISAN seal strictly adheres to governmental metallurgical compliance:

  • Royal Heritage Line (Kundan & Jadau): Forged with jewellers brass cores, lined with genuine 24K pure gold leaf foil (Kundan), and set with certified uncut stones and hydro-gemstone tumblers.
  • Jaipuri Meenakari Bangles: Authentic vitreous mineral enamel fired at 800°C kiln temperatures. Fitted with solid brass screw-locking mechanisms.
  • Anti-Tarnish Demi-Fine: Certified surgical-grade 316L titanium steel bonded with authentic 18-karat gold via aerospace Physical Vapor Deposition (PVD). 100% hypoallergenic, nickel-free, and lead-free.
  • Feeling Chhail Silver Collection: Hallmarked 925 German silver with authentic antique oxidized patina.

2 Lifetime Anti-Tarnish Guarantee Terms

Our 18K PVD gold pieces carry an unconditional Lifetime Color Warranty against rusting, blackening, or turning skin green under normal daily use (including contact with water, sweat, and mild soaps). Should your piece exhibit premature metal discoloration under standard wear, AURA & ARTISAN will replug or replace the item free of charge. This warranty excludes severe mechanical abrasion or deliberate chemical etching.

3 Handcrafted Karigari Nuances & Variance

Please note that our Meenakari and Pachi Kundan creations are individually handcrafted by master artisans in Jaipur. Subtle variations in brushwork shading, mineral enamel depth, or natural gemstone veining are not defects; they are the authentic hallmark of human artistry that makes each heirloom singular and non-identical.

4 White-Glove Insured Shipping & Doorstep Delivery

Every consignment is shipped inside a tamper-proof security box sealed with our proprietary holographic tamper-evident tape. All shipments are 100% insured in transit against theft or carrier loss until signed receipt by the recipient. Customers are advised to inspect the exterior holographic seal before signing for high-value bridal consignments.

5 7-Day Doorstep Returns & Bangle Size Exchanges

We offer a seamless 7-Day Doorstep Exchange Policy. If your handcrafted kada size requires adjustment (e.g. from 2.4 to 2.6), or if you wish to exchange a piece, initiate a request via our concierge. Items must be returned in their original velvet-lined presentation box with security tags intact. Custom monogrammed engravings and personalized bespoke bridal sets are non-refundable.

6 Jurisdiction & Maison Concierge

Any legal dispute arising from purchases shall be governed by the laws of India, subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Jaipur, Rajasthan. For corporate inquiries, bespoke salon bookings, or warranty assistance, contact our legal team at legal@auraandartisan.com.
